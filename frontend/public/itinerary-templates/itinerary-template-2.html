Architect's planning notes

🏔Altitude: Chitkul sits at 3,450 m. We've kept Day 5 a half-day visit only — no overnight — so Priya acclimatises gradually from Sangla (2,680 m) first. Carry Diamox as a precaution; confirm with your doctor before travel.
🛣Roads: The Sangla–Chitkul road is narrow and single-lane in sections. Our driver (Mohan) has done this route 40+ times. No overtaking on the cliff-side stretches.
🍎Season: October is the apple-harvest window. Narkanda and Kalpa orchards will be in full swing — Mohan will stop at a roadside stall each morning for fresh-picked fruit.
📶Connectivity: BSNL is the only network with coverage beyond Sangla. Airtel/Jio cut out from Narkanda onwards. Download offline maps before departing Shimla.
🌦Weather: October nights drop to 2–4°C at Chitkul and Kalpa. Pack a down jacket and thermal base layer even if the days feel mild.
