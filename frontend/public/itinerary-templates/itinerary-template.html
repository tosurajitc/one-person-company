Architect's planning notes

👴Nana's comfort: Every hotel has been selected for ground-floor or lift access. All lunch spots are shack-style with covered seating — no uneven cobblestones or long walks from the car. Driving stretches are capped at 90 min with a rest stop.
🏊Aanya's swim safety: Palolem's crescent bay has the calmest water in South Goa — the headland breaks ocean swell. We've checked the Dec lifeguard schedule; flags will be green. Aanya should stay inside the flag zone. Arm floats optional at her age — your call.
🌊Currents & tides: Baga beach has rip currents in Dec afternoon. Aanya should swim only in the lifeguard zone and not past the buoy line. Agonda (Day 6) has no lifeguard — strictly ankle-deep for everyone that morning.
🚗Transport: We've booked an AC Toyota Innova for the full 6 days. Goa's auto-rickshaws are fun but the roads from Panaji to South Goa are 2.5 hrs — not suitable for Nana. Driver (Dominic) speaks Hindi and English.
🎄Christmas week: 22–25 Dec is peak season. Palolem restaurants fill up on Christmas Eve — we've pre-booked the beach dinner at Magic Italy. North Goa beaches are crowded on 25 Dec; our Baga day is 23 Dec, ahead of the rush.
💊Health: Carry Nana's full prescription list. Nearest good hospital is Goa Medical College, Panaji (Day 1–2 base). In South Goa, Apollo Clinic Margao is 20 min from Palolem. Dominic has both numbers saved.
