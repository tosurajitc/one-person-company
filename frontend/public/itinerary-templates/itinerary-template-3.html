Architect's planning notes

🦏Kaziranga safari: Booked the Central Range morning jeep safari for Day 3 (best rhino sightings) and Western Range for Day 4 morning. Both slots fill 6–8 weeks ahead in Feb — confirmed and paid. Carry the booking PDF on your phone.
🌿Double-decker root bridge trek: 7 km round trip, ~4 hrs, ~2,400 steps each way — mostly descending on the way in, steep climb out. Carry at least 1.5 L water. Start no later than 8 AM to avoid afternoon heat. Trekking poles recommended; available to rent in Tyrna village (₹50).
📷Dawki photography: The Umngot is clearest in the morning before 10 AM — schedule the boat ride for 8–9 AM. The Feb dry season is the best window; water turns turbid post-monsoon. Golden hour is at 5:30 PM on the riverbank.
📶Connectivity: Jio and Airtel work in Guwahati, Kaziranga and Shillong. Signal drops in the Cherrapunji valleys and is near-zero at Dawki. Download Google Maps offline for the Meghalaya section before leaving Shillong.
🛂ILP / permits: No Inner Line Permit needed for Assam or Meghalaya — you need one only for Arunachal, Nagaland, Mizoram and Manipur. Carry Aadhaar for hotel check-ins; some properties in Meghalaya still ask for it.
🚗Transport: A single cab (Baba Travels, Guwahati-based) covers the full circuit — Guwahati → Kaziranga → Shillong → Cherrapunji → Dawki → Guwahati airport. Driver (Bijoy) has done this loop 80+ times. Long drive days (Kaziranga → Shillong, ~4.5 hrs) have rest stops built in.
